Partnership Framework Blueprint

BITDA & Ultradigital Technologies Ltd Joint Mandate

Proposal for modernizing technology licensing, device tagging, and market regulation in Benue State.

Role of BITDA (Regulatory Authority)

  • Policy direction, statutory enforcement coordination, and legislative compliance.
  • Revenue administration and official gazetting of tech regulations.
  • Executive oversight and liaison with Benue State House of Assembly & Governor's Office.
  • Joint inspection and confiscation of non-compliant substandard gadgets.

Role of Ultradigital Tech Ltd (Technical Partner)

  • System Architecture, Cloud Infrastructure, and Platform Web Development.
  • Secure Database Engine development with real-time analytics dashboards.
  • Digital QR Verification, mobile app integration, and cybersecurity defense.
  • Capacity building, staff upskilling, and technical maintenance.
